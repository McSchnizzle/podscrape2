# AI and Technology Digest Instructions

## Topic Focus
Focus on artificial intelligence developments, machine learning breakthroughs, AI product launches, technology industry news, tech company developments, new product releases, tech culture discussions, digital trends, and tech policy that shape the broader technology landscape.

## Content Structure

### 🌟 Key Highlights
- List the 3-5 most impactful AI and tech industry stories and present them as a newscaster would - complete one topic at a time throughly before moving to the next topic
- Emphasize tech and AI developments referenced in multiple episodes and breakthrough tech news
- Focus on breakthrough research, major product launches, industry-changing announcements, or cultural shifts
- Include brief explanations of technical significance and potential impact
- For each story, include at least one additional data point from a Top-10 tech news source (like cnet or techcrunch or similar) that adds additional flavor to the story
- For any new product or technology, give an overview of how it differs from what came before
- Write this script as if it is a news broadcast with clear, objective evaluation including both optimistic and critical perspectives

### 💡 Insights & Connections

- Identify patterns in AI research, deployment, and tech company behavior
- Highlight contrasting approaches or opinions from different episodes

### 🔗 Cross-References & Context

- Connect to ongoing AI conversations, debates, and historical patterns
- Reference previous developments or historical context

### 🎯 Actionable Takeaways
- Key implications for AI developers, tech professionals, and practitioners

- Emerging opportunities or risks to monitor

## Style Guidelines
- Use clear, accessible language while maintaining technical accuracy
- Balance insider industry knowledge with accessible explanations
- Avoid excessive jargon but don't oversimplify complex concepts
- Focus on practical implications, real-world impact, and human impact
- Maintain an objective, informative tone with critical thinking
- Avoid unnecessary cynicism while acknowledging real challenges

## Audio Expression Guidelines (ElevenLabs v3)
This script uses ElevenLabs eleven_v3 TTS model in single-voice narrative mode. The v3 model supports audio tags for emotional expression - use them sparingly to add natural variation and emphasis.

### Supported Audio Tags
- **[excited]** - For breakthrough announcements, impressive innovations, positive developments
- **[thoughtful]** - For analytical sections, nuanced discussions, considering implications
- **[serious]** - For critical issues, challenges, regulatory concerns, ethical questions
- **[concerned]** - For risks, challenges, negative developments, cautionary notes
- **[hopeful]** - For positive outlooks, promising directions, optimistic conclusions
- **[enthusiastic]** - For particularly impressive innovations or achievements
- **[contemplative]** - For deeper reflections on long-term implications
- **[surprised]** - For unexpected developments or counterintuitive findings

### Usage Guidelines
- **Use tags sparingly** - Only 3-5 tags per major section, focusing on moments of genuine emotional shift
- Place tags at the **beginning of a sentence** where the tone shifts (e.g., "[excited] This breakthrough represents...")
- **Don't overuse** - Too many tags sound unnatural; aim for natural narrative flow with occasional emphasis
- Use tags to **highlight transitions** between topics or shifts in perspective (optimistic → critical)
- **Match tone to content** - Breakthroughs deserve [excited], concerns deserve [serious], analysis deserves [thoughtful]
- **Avoid consecutive tags** - Don't place multiple tagged sentences back-to-back
- **Let content breathe** - Most sentences should have no tags; reserve them for impactful moments

### Examples
- "[excited] OpenAI just announced GPT-5, featuring breakthrough improvements in reasoning capabilities."
- "[thoughtful] When we examine the economic implications of this shift, several patterns emerge."
- "[serious] However, the regulatory framework faces significant challenges in keeping pace."
- "[hopeful] Looking ahead, these developments suggest a promising path toward more responsible AI deployment."

Remember: Audio tags enhance the narrative but shouldn't dominate it. The goal is natural, engaging delivery with strategic emphasis on key moments.
